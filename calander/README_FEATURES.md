# Heatmap Calendar + Auto Scheduler (Smart Focus Calendar)

## Overview

This project combines two powerful calendar features into one intelligent scheduling tool: the Heatmap Calendar and the Auto Scheduler (Smart Focus Calendar). The Heatmap Calendar visualizes your activity intensity over time, helping you identify productive and less productive periods at a glance. The Auto Scheduler leverages this data, along with your preferences and priorities, to automatically create an optimized daily schedule that maximizes focus and efficiency.

Together, these features provide users a holistic view of their time usage and a smart, adaptive way to plan their work, ensuring better time management and improved productivity.

## Why it Beats Competitors

- **Data-Driven Insights:** Unlike traditional calendars, the Heatmap Calendar visually highlights your productivity trends, making it easy to spot patterns and adjust your habits accordingly.
- **Automated Scheduling:** The Auto Scheduler intelligently creates schedules based on your priority tasks and historical focus data, removing the hassle of manual planning.
- **Smart Focus Optimization:** By analyzing when you are most productive, the scheduler prioritizes tasks during peak focus times, leading to more effective work sessions.
- **User-Friendly Integration:** Both features integrate seamlessly into a single interface, giving you real-time feedback and automatic adjustments without switching apps.
- **Customizable & Adaptive:** The system adapts to your unique workflow and can evolve as your habits change, unlike rigid scheduling tools.

## How to Use

1. **View Your Heatmap:** Access the calendar heatmap to see a color-coded overview of your activity intensity over days, weeks, or months.
2. **Input Your Tasks:** Add tasks with priorities and estimated durations into the scheduler.
3. **Run Auto Scheduler:** Let the system generate an optimized daily plan that fits your high-productivity windows.
4. **Follow Your Schedule:** Use the suggested schedule to guide your work sessions, focusing when you are naturally more alert.
5. **Monitor and Adjust:** Review your heatmap regularly to track improvements and make manual adjustments if needed.
6. **Sync and Export:** Optionally sync with external calendars and export your schedule summaries for reporting or sharing.
